Why Kolmari

One workspace instead of twenty browser tabs

You can research a move on your own — people do it every day. The problem is never a lack of information. It's that the information is scattered, contradictory, and never tied to your situation.

Piecing it together yourself

  • ✕A dozen government sites, blogs, and Reddit threads that quietly disagree with each other.
  • ✕Visa rules you're not certain you read correctly — or that changed since the post was written.
  • ✕Cost-of-living guesses that ignore your household size and income.
  • ✕Notes spread across tabs, screenshots, and documents you can't find later.
  • ✕No clear first step, and no real sense of what's achievable by when.

With Kolmari

  • ✓Every destination compared with the same numbers behind every screen.
  • ✓The visa routes you already qualify for, checked against your profile.
  • ✓A monthly budget tuned to your household, income, and city.
  • ✓Documents, tasks, and a timeline held together in one plan.
  • ✓A clear first step — and a target month to work backwards from.

Built to be trusted

Where our information comes from

You're making a major life decision. You should know where the numbers come from and how current they are before you rely on them.

Official sources first

Visa rules, income thresholds and timelines trace back to government immigration and consular pages — the primary source, not a summary of a summary.

One consistent model

Cost of living, budgets and country comparisons all run on the same underlying data, so the figures line up from a country page to your plan.

Reviewed as rules change

Immigration rules move, so research is maintained over time rather than published once — and we're upfront about what's a firm requirement versus a typical range.

Guidance, not legal advice

Kolmari helps you compare, plan and prepare. For the final filing we point you to the official route and, where it matters, a qualified professional.
